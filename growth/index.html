Straight answers

Before you ask

Do you need my password?
No — and this is the single most important thing on this page. Everything is delivered to your public handle, page or video link. If any seller anywhere asks for your login to grow your account, stop talking to them.
How long before it starts?
Most orders begin moving within 1 to 6 hours of being confirmed. Larger packages are then paced out deliberately over the following days rather than delivered all at once.
What if followers drop off afterwards?
Every order carries a 30-day refill. If any of the delivery falls away inside that window, message us and we top it back up at no cost.
How and when do I pay?
MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or cash at our office in Kampala. 30% starts the order and the balance is due once delivery is visibly running — you are never asked to pay in full for something you haven't seen yet.
Do you deliver outside Uganda?
Yes. We serve Uganda, South Sudan and DR Congo. Prices show in UGX or USD depending on where you tell us you are — you can change that any time with the Change link under the price above.
Can I grow more than one account?
Yes. Orders are placed per account, so you can run as many as you need. If you want Instagram, TikTok and Facebook together, the all-three package covers 10,000 followers on each and saves you $50 against ordering them separately.
I want a website too — do you do that?
We do. That's the other half of what we build — see the website packages, starting at $500 with a business email included.
