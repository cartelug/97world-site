    Straight answers

    Before you ask

    Do you need my password?
    No. Everything is delivered to your public username or content link. We will never ask for your login.
    When does delivery start?
    Most orders begin moving within 1 to 6 hours of being confirmed, then continue gradually rather than landing all at once.
    What does refill mean?
    For eligible services, if the delivered amount drops within 30 days, we top it back up at no cost. We say clearly which services this applies to before you order.
    What happens if followers drop?
    For a refill-eligible service, message us within the 30-day window and we replace what was lost, free. For a quote-only service, the exact refill terms are confirmed when we send you the price.
    How do I pay?
    MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or cash at our office in Kampala. Where a deposit applies, it starts the order and the balance is due once delivery is visibly running.
    Can I order outside Uganda?
    Yes — we also serve South Sudan and DR Congo. Prices show in UGX or USD depending on your region, changeable any time with the Change link near the price.
