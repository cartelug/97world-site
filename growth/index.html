08 / QUESTIONS

The details, clearly answered.

Do I need to send my password?
No. Send your public profile link or username only. Do not send account passwords, email passwords, or verification codes.
What happens before I pay?
We check your links, confirm the package and payment terms, and ask you to approve the order. Payment instructions come after that approval.
Do I pay everything upfront?
No. The Growth flow shown here uses 50% to start and 50% once delivery is visibly running on your account.
Will I receive a receipt and updates?
A paid order is confirmed with a receipt or order reference. We share progress updates on WhatsApp and you can use the order tracking link where available.
Is this organic growth?
These are paid promotional engagement services. We do not promise organic followers, customers, sales, virality, or a permanent result.
Can I order from outside Uganda?
The live catalog also serves South Sudan and DR Congo. Use the currency and region menu when building an order, or ask us on WhatsApp.
