Questions

Do you need my password?
No. Everything is delivered to a public username or link. We will never ask for your login.
How does Combo pricing work?
It is a pricing mode, not a different product. Select three platforms and the whole order comes down 10%, four platforms 12.5%, five or more 15%. The saving is taken off the real total of what you picked — there is no inflated "before" price.
When does delivery start?
Most orders begin moving within 1 to 6 hours of being confirmed, then continue gradually rather than landing all at once.
What does refill cover?
Where a service includes refill, anything that drops within 30 days is topped back up free. It applies to followers, subscribers and members — things that can actually drop. One-off metrics like views and plays cannot drop, so they are not covered.
How do I pay?
MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or cash at our office in Kampala. You confirm the order on WhatsApp first and we take payment there — nothing is charged on this page.
Can I order from outside Uganda?
Yes — we also serve South Sudan and DR Congo, priced in USD. Switch currency from the button in the header.
