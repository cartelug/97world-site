The terms. In plain language.

How quote requests become projects, and how scope, payment, delivery, revisions and ownership work.

Last updated · 10 August 2026

1. Business and contact details

97 World is a digital design and technology studio operating from Kampala, Uganda and Juba, South Sudan. The contracting supplier, service address and any registration or tax details that apply will be identified on the written quotation or invoice before you accept a project.

2. Quote requests and contract formation

Website prices and automated estimates are invitations to request a quotation, not binding offers. Sending the start form opens WhatsApp with a draft message; nothing is sent until you send it. A contract begins only when 97 World confirms the scope, deliverables, total price, currency, applicable taxes or fees, payment schedule and delivery timetable in writing and you accept those terms.

Before accepting, you can review and correct the request, remove services or withdraw without charge. The final written quotation controls if it differs from an automated estimate or currency conversion.

3. Services and scope

We provide websites, landing pages, brand systems, logos, campaign design, social content, audience-growth plans and custom software or automation. Your written quotation identifies the exact deliverables, included revision rounds, support period and any third-party costs. Work outside that scope requires your approval of a change quote.

4. Payment and project start

Unless the written quotation says otherwise, projects use a 50/50 schedule: a 50% deposit reserves the slot and the balance is due at the agreed delivery or handover milestone. Production starts after the deposit and required content are received. Payment instructions are confirmed directly; we never ask for PINs or passwords.

5. Delivery, review and acceptance

The delivery estimate begins when the deposit, brief and required content are complete. Client response delays, missing content or approved scope changes may move the timetable. Unless a different period is agreed, we will perform within the period required by applicable law. We will notify you if a service becomes unavailable and provide any remedy required by law.

You must review milestones and report objective defects within the review period shown in the quotation. Preference changes and new requests are revisions or change requests rather than defects.

6. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for accurate instructions, timely feedback and the legality and rights status of copy, logos, images, data or other material you supply. You must not request unlawful, deceptive, infringing or abusive work. Domains, hosting, software subscriptions, advertising spend and platform accounts remain your responsibility unless the quotation expressly includes them.

7. Cancellation and refunds

We honor all mandatory cancellation and refund rights under applicable law. Any additional cancellation terms will appear in the written quotation and do not exclude mandatory consumer rights. If you ask us to begin a personalized service immediately, the quotation will record that request and explain how it affects cancellation rights. If we cannot deliver the agreed service, we will repair, replace, reperform or refund the affected part as applicable.

8. Ownership, licences and portfolio use

You keep ownership of materials you supply. After full payment, ownership of bespoke final deliverables transfers as stated in the quotation. 97 World keeps its pre-existing tools, reusable methods, licensed fonts, stock assets, frameworks and general know-how; you receive the licence needed to use any such elements included in your deliverable. We may show completed public work in our portfolio unless you opt out in writing.

9. Growth services and third-party platforms

Audience-growth work does not guarantee revenue, rankings, engagement quality or permanent follower counts, because third-party platforms control distribution, moderation and account rules. Clients must follow the applicable platform terms. 97 World is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta, WhatsApp or other platforms merely because their services are used.

10. Confidentiality and privacy

Each party will use reasonable care with non-public project information and use it only for the project, except where disclosure is authorized or legally required. Our handling of personal information is described in the Privacy Policy.

11. Warranties and responsibility

We will perform the agreed services with reasonable care and correct verified defects within the agreed review period. To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is responsible for indirect or consequential loss that was not reasonably foreseeable. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability arising from fraud or wilful misconduct, or removes mandatory consumer rights.

12. Governing law and disputes

These terms and project quotations are governed by the laws of Uganda unless the written quotation states another applicable law. If a dispute arises, both parties will first try to resolve it in good faith through the project contact channel. Either party may then use the courts or any mandatory consumer complaint process available under applicable law.

13. Changes and contact

Updates apply to quote requests made after the updated date above, not to an existing project unless both parties agree in writing. Questions can be sent to hello@the97.world or +256 708 735 878.
