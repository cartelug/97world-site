The terms. In plain language.

What you're agreeing to when you order from 97 World — deposits, delivery, revisions and ownership. No fine print, no surprises.

Last updated · 10 August 2026

1. Acceptance of terms

By sending an order to 97 World — on WhatsApp or via this site's start form — you agree to these terms. If anything here doesn't work for you, tell us before the deposit lands; we'd rather sort it out upfront than after.

2. What we build

97 World is the design sector of 97 World: websites, landing pages, brand kits, logos, campaign fliers, business cards, social packs, audience growth plans and custom systems. Every quote is scoped to what's listed on the pricing page or agreed with you directly on WhatsApp — no hidden extras added after the fact.

3. Quotes and pricing

Prices shown on this site are real, current quotes in UGX or USD. A quote is fixed once we confirm your brief on WhatsApp; if the scope changes materially afterward (extra pages, a new deliverable, a rushed deadline), we'll requote that part before continuing.

4. Deposits and payment

Every project runs on a 50/50 plan: a 50% deposit locks your slot and starts the build clock, and the balance is due on delivery. We do not begin production work before the deposit is confirmed. Accepted payment methods are shared with you on WhatsApp once your country and quote are set.

5. Delivery and revisions

Turnaround times quoted per service (see Services & Prices) start from deposit confirmation, assuming timely replies and content from your side. Each service includes a reasonable revision loop during the build; revisions requested after final handover are quoted separately.

6. Refunds and cancellations

If you cancel before work has started, your deposit is refunded in full. Once production has started, the deposit covers the work already done and is non-refundable; we'll always tell you honestly how far along a project is if you're considering cancelling. If we fail to deliver what was agreed, we will fix it or refund the affected portion — we stand behind what we build.

7. Ownership and handover

On full and final payment, ownership of the delivered files (site code, design source files, brand assets) transfers to you. Until the balance clears, delivered files remain 97 World's property. We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

8. Your responsibilities

You're responsible for the accuracy of the content, copy, images and information you provide, and for having the rights to any material you ask us to use. Domains, hosting and third-party subscriptions you choose to keep are managed and paid for by you after handover unless we've agreed otherwise in writing.

9. Communication

Projects are run direct on WhatsApp — that's where briefs, approvals and updates happen, and it's the record we both rely on if a question comes up later.

10. Changes to these terms

If these terms change, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Changes apply to orders placed after the update — not to projects already in progress.

11. Contact

Questions about these terms — reach us on WhatsApp, the same place you'd send an order.
