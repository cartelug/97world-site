# Snapchat service artwork

Five generated 3D editorial illustrations for the 97 Growth service selector.
Palette: Snapchat yellow, ivory, warm white and charcoal. No prices, statistics,
customer endorsements or delivery promises are baked into the illustrations.
The official platform logo remains a separate asset.

| Asset | Creative brief |
| --- | --- |
| snapchat-followers-v1.webp | Porcelain and smoked-glass profiles orbiting a radiant yellow sphere: community. |
| snapchat-refill-v1.webp | A yellow continuous ribbon surrounding porcelain busts: replenishment. |
| snapchat-likes-v1.webp | Tactile yellow and smoked-glass hearts: content appreciation. |
| snapchat-story-v1.webp | Layered vertical landscape frames and viewing halos: public stories. |
| snapchat-spotlight-v1.webp | A video frame, yellow play triangle and overhead light: Spotlight content. |

Generated 11 November 2026. Shared prompt direction: premium 3D still life,
landscape 3:2, right-weighted composition, quiet ivory negative space, soft studio
lighting, realistic materials; no text, digits, logos, watermarks or fake analytics.
Spotlight was refined to remove a generated mascot and use a neutral glass sphere.

WebP delivery copies are 1200 × 800. The form loads only the selected scene,
reuses the cached image for its faint page backdrop, cancels outdated image
callbacks on rapid changes, and honours reduced-motion preferences.
