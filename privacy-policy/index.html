Your data. Handled straight.

What happens to information you choose to share while requesting a quote, working with us or browsing this site.

Last updated · 10 August 2026

1. Who is responsible

For this notice, 97 World is responsible for the personal information you choose to provide through the97.world or in a project conversation. We are a digital design and technology studio operating from Kampala, Uganda and Juba, South Sudan.

Privacy emailhello@the97.world Phone / WhatsApp+256 708 735 878 Websitethe97.world Operating locationsKampala · Juba

2. Information covered by this notice

We limit collection to information needed to answer an enquiry or deliver a project:

  • Quote and contact details — name, phone number, country, selected services, project brief, timeline, payment-method preference and anything else you include.
  • Project records — proposals, approvals, content, design references, deliverables and support conversations.
  • Payment evidence — transaction references or confirmation screenshots. We never ask for mobile-money PINs, passwords or card security codes.
  • Browser preferences — the country and services you select are stored in your browser so the quote can continue between pages. This information stays on your device unless you include it in a WhatsApp message.
  • Technical requests — GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may process IP address, browser and request-log information for delivery and security under its own privacy terms.

The country, selected-service and pricing-step preferences remain in essential browser storage until you clear site data; a page-transition flag lasts only for the current browser session. These values are used only to preserve your quote and navigation state, not to track you across websites.

3. What happens when you use WhatsApp

The start form creates a WhatsApp link containing the details you entered and your displayed estimate. Nothing is sent by this website. If you open WhatsApp and send the message, WhatsApp/Meta receives your phone number, profile information and conversation and may process it outside Uganda under the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. You can instead contact us by email or phone.

4. Why we use information

We use information to respond to your request, prepare and confirm a quote, form and perform a project agreement, take payment, deliver and support the work, keep required accounting records, protect the service and handle disputes. The legal reasons we rely on are your consent where requested, steps you ask us to take before a contract, performance of a contract and compliance with law.

Name, contact, country and project details are needed to provide a useful quote; without them we may be unable to respond. “How did you hear about us?” and extra notes are optional. We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising. Marketing messages require a separate choice and can be stopped at any time.

5. Who may receive it

Information may be handled by WhatsApp/Meta for messaging, GitHub/Microsoft for site hosting and security, payment or accounting providers used for your project, and professional advisers where reasonably necessary. Providers receive only what is needed for their role. Some providers operate outside Uganda; we use them only where an appropriate legal basis and safeguards apply.

6. Retention

  • Unsuccessful quote enquiries: up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Project working files and routine support conversations: up to 24 months after handover, unless a longer support period is agreed.
  • Contracts, invoices, payment and tax records: at least five years after the relevant tax period, or longer where an unresolved dispute or legal proceeding requires it.

After the applicable period, information is deleted or de-identified where reasonably possible.

7. Your rights

You may ask whether we hold information about you, request access, ask who received it, correct inaccurate information, request deletion where applicable, object to harmful processing or direct marketing, and ask for review of a decision made solely by automated means. We may verify your identity before acting and may retain records the law requires.

You may also raise a complaint with Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office.

8. Security and incidents

We use reasonable access controls, device security, account protection and limited staff access. No online service can promise absolute security. If we believe personal information has been accessed or acquired without authority, we will follow applicable notification duties.

9. Children and sensitive information

Our services are directed to adults and businesses. Children should not submit information without a parent or guardian's authorization. Do not send passwords, PINs, payment credentials, health information or other sensitive information through the quote form or WhatsApp unless we specifically request it for a lawful purpose.

10. Changes and contact

We will post material updates here and change the date above. An updated notice applies from its publication date; it does not turn unrelated processing into consent. For access, correction, deletion or any privacy question, email hello@the97.world or contact +256 708 735 878.
