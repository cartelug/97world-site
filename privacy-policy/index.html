Home / Privacy Policy

Your data. Handled straight.

What 97 World collects when you order on WhatsApp or browse this site, why, and how it's protected — no fine print.

Last updated · 10 August 2026

1. Who we are

97 World is a design and technology studio operating from Kampala, Uganda and Juba, South Sudan. This policy covers the97world.com and every order placed with us on WhatsApp. If you have questions about it, message us directly — the same WhatsApp number you'd use to order.

2. What we collect

We keep this to what an order actually needs:

  • Order details — your name, WhatsApp number, country, the service you want and any brief, references or budget notes you send us.
  • Payment confirmation — deposit and balance screenshots or references you share to confirm a payment; we do not process card or mobile money payments ourselves, and we never ask for PINs or passwords.
  • Basic site usage — this site is a static site with no visitor analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser may cache pages locally (a "service worker") purely to make repeat visits faster; nothing from that cache is sent to us.

3. How we use it

Order information is used to scope your project, quote it, build it, deliver it and follow up on payment — nothing else. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where it's stored

Order conversations live in WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own privacy and encryption terms. Where we log a new order internally for our own tracking (for example, into a private spreadsheet), only the order details above are recorded — never payment credentials.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as reasonably needed to deliver your project, support it afterward, and meet basic bookkeeping needs. You can ask us to delete your order history at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.

6. Your choices

You can ask us — on WhatsApp, any time — to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. We'll act on that within a reasonable time, unless we're required to keep something for legal or accounting reasons.

7. Third parties

We use WhatsApp to communicate and this site is hosted on GitHub Pages; both process data under their own privacy terms. We do not embed third-party analytics, ad trackers or social widgets on this site.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to order from us after a change means you accept the update.

9. Contact

Questions about your data or this policy — reach us on WhatsApp, the same place you'd send an order.
