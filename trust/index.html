Trust Centre

Let's talk about scams.

You're right to be careful with money online. So here is how to check whether anyone asking you to pay first is real — us included. Use it on us. Then use it on the next person who quotes you a price.

Five questions

How to check any seller

None of these require you to trust us. They're just the questions that separate a business from a disappearing act.

1. Can you find them away from the internet?

A real business has somewhere to be and a number a person picks up. We're in Kampala, and the number on this site is answered by a person, not a bot. Call it before you pay anything — that's what it's for.

2. Do they ask for your password?

Nobody needs your login to add followers to a public account. Anyone who asks is either careless with your security or planning to take the account. We deliver to your public handle or link, and we will never ask you for a password.

3. Can you talk to their customers?

Not the reviews printed on their own website — anyone can type those. Actual customers, in a place where they can say what they like. Ours are in the VIP group below, and they'll tell you the truth whether it flatters us or not.

4. Do they want all the money upfront?

Paying in full before you've seen anything is where most of the money that disappears, disappears. We start your order on 50%, and the balance is only due once delivery is visibly running on your account.

5. What happens when something goes wrong?

Ask this before you pay, not after. If the answer is vague, that's your answer. Ours: every order carries a 30-day refill — anything that drops off inside 30 days gets topped back up, free.
