Straight answers

Before you ask

What does the $500 actually cover?
The design, build and launch of your site, plus a business email on your own domain. The exact page list is agreed with you before anything starts, so you know precisely what you're getting for the money.
Can I pay in instalments?
$250 starts the work and the balance is due on delivery, once the site is built and you've seen it. If you need a different arrangement, ask — it's a conversation, not a form.
How long does it take?
It depends on how many pages you need and how quickly you get us your content — photos, prices, the text about your business. We agree a delivery date with you upfront, before you pay anything.
What if I already have a domain?
Bring it — we'll work with what you already have. If you don't have one yet, we'll sort that out together as part of getting you live.
Will I be able to update it myself?
Tell us what you'll want to change regularly — prices, photos, opening hours — and we'll set it up so you can. Agree it with us before we start and it gets built in from the beginning.
Do you handle the social media side too?
We do — that's the other half of what we build. See growth packages for followers on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube.
